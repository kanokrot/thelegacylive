# Sources

- **Isan administrative names**: province, amphoe, and tambon names in `isan-tambon.json` are a static subset of [kongvut/thai-province-data](https://github.com/kongvut/thai-province-data), API `api/latest/province_with_district_and_sub_district.json`, downloaded 2026-10-20. The upstream repository declares MIT © 2025 Kongvut Sangkla; the bundled file keeps only names for the 20 Isan provinces. Story villages such as `บ้านโนนคำ` are generated fictional names and do not describe real settlements.

- **gpp**: Gross provincial product per capita, current prices, 2023 (provisional), baht/person/year. NESDC via NSO Statistical Yearbook 2025, Table 10.14. https://www.nso.go.th/public/e-book/Statistical-Yearbook/SYB-2025_webPage/File%20SYB/2025/10/T%2010.14%202025.pdf
- **income**: Household monthly income 2023 (mean/median, total and regular, per household and per person), household size; sample-survey estimates. NSO, Household Socio-economic Survey 2023, Income and Income Distribution of Households 2023 (provincial level), Table 3. https://www.nso.go.th/public/e-book/Analytical-Reports/Income-2566/46/ and /47/
- **debt**: Mean debt (baht) of households that have debt, 2023. same report, Table 16. https://www.nso.go.th/public/e-book/Analytical-Reports/Income-2566/95/
- **poverty**: Poverty headcount ratio (expenditure) 2023: country 3.41%, Northeast 4.16%; 2024 country 4.89%. NSO Statistical Yearbook 2025 Table 8.8; NESDC via Bangkok Biz News. https://www.nso.go.th/public/e-book/Statistical-Yearbook/SYB-2025_webPage/File%20SYB/2025/8/T%208.8%202025.pdf

## Not available (do not invent)
- Per-province life expectancy, under-5 mortality, and per-province poverty rate for all 20 provinces: no official figures found.
- Values labelled 'ค่าจำลอง' in the game are random rolls, not statistics.
- GPP is provisional (2023p). Household figures are sample-survey estimates and may carry sampling error.

## Isan family and money events

Accessed 2026-10-20. These facts provide broad context for fictional, unnamed households; they do not describe any one family or village.

- **FM01**: In 2023, 60.8% of Northeast households reported having debt; the mean debt among indebted households was about 329,767 baht. This is a regional survey estimate, not a debt level assigned to an individual family. NSO, Household Income and Income Distribution 2023, provincial tables. https://www.nso.go.th/public/e-book/Analytical-Reports/Income-2566/144/
- **FM02**: NSO's Northeast household analysis compares income, spending and debt across household economic groups, and shows that agriculture is common among the lowest-income group. NSO, Grassroots Households 2019, Northeast tables. https://www.nso.go.th/public/e-book/Analytical-Reports/Foundation_household_62/110/
- **FM03**: A 2019 child and family survey found that 46.3% of Thai children aged 0–17 did not live with both birth parents; the report says the share was highest in the Northeast and describes relatives, including grandparents, caring for children in some families. NSO and UNICEF, Children and Youth 2021. https://www.nso.go.th/public/e-book/Analytical-Reports/Children_Youth_64/22-23/
- **FM04**: NESDC describes households where older people raise grandchildren without parents present and notes that this arrangement is most common in the Northeast among the regions it compares. - **FM05**: The Rice Department's crop-year plan separates the main rice season from the off-season period, giving a broad calendar for planning farm work. - **FM06**: The Thai Meteorological Department explains that dry spells during the rainy season, including around June–July, can affect agriculture, and identifies central parts of the Northeast among areas vulnerable to drought. NSO, Monthly household expenses by category and region, first half of 2023. https://www.nso.go.th/nsoweb/storage/survey_detail/2023/20231102091434_53415.pdf
- **FM09**: Ministry of Education materials identify travel, food and learning supplies as costs that can make it harder for children in low-income households to continue studying. The cards use these as possible household trade-offs, not a fixed cost or universal outcome. Ministry of Education, education policy report. https://www.sp.moe.go.th/web_sp_68/report_files/moe2725681_10_6801dd8963d8b1.93527009.pdf

## Mortality data research (accessed 2026-10-21)

- **MORT01**: HISO's *การวิเคราะห์ข้อมูลตาย 13 สาเหตุ* uses registered deaths coded by the Ministry of Public Health and population denominators, grouped by registered address. Cause-specific mortality rate is deaths multiplied by 100,000 divided by midyear population. The report presents sex and age-group comparisons for 2023 and health-zone comparisons for 2023; the age groups do not match all requested game bands. HISO, 2023 data report, method and cause list. https://www.hiso.or.th/healthoutcome/report/%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A213%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B2%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%B8.pdf
- **MORT02**: HISO 2023 reports national cause-specific rates and selected sex/age summaries and health-zone rates for COPD, liver disease, diabetes, ischemic heart disease, stroke, chronic kidney disease, liver and bile-duct cancer, lung cancer, colorectal cancer, breast cancer, road traffic injury, drowning and suicide. The confirmed figures recorded in `mortality-reference.json` are descriptive observations, not direct game probabilities. Same HISO report as MORT01.
- **MORT03**: NSO Statistical Yearbook Thailand 2026 includes deaths by sex and age at death for 2022–2024, crude death rate by region/province for 2023–2024, and infant deaths/rates by region and sex. Its infant table is under age one and does not separate neonatal causes such as prematurity. NSO, Statistical Yearbook Thailand 2026, tables 1.13–1.15. https://www.nso.go.th/public/e-book/Statistical-Yearbook/SYB-2026/22/
- **MORT04**: NSO's 2025 gender report cites life expectancy at birth in 2024 of 71.9 years for males and 80.0 years for females, based on the Mahidol Population Gazette. This is a national calibration reference, not an Isan life table or annual age-specific death probability. NSO, Report on Gender Statistics 2025. https://www.nso.go.th/public/e-book/Analytical-Reports/Report_Gender_2025/21/
- **MORT05**: The DDC drowning dashboard reports child drowning deaths by age, sex, year and health zone. The table's event-registration definition differs from HISO's death-registration mortality-rate series, so counts and rates must not be combined without matching denominators and definitions. DDC, Drowning surveillance dashboard. https://drowningreport.ddc.moph.go.th/drowningdashboard/public/index.php/deathcert
- **MORT06**: DDC's road-death open dataset contains individual records including age, sex, province, year and selected risk fields. - **MORT07**: NSO Table 1.13 reports registered death counts by sex and five-year age groups (with 0–4 and 65+ outer groups) for 2021–2023; Table 1.4 reports registered population by matching age bands and sex. The model's sourced annual rate inputs divide 2023 deaths by 2023 population in each matching band. NSO, Statistical Yearbook Thailand 2025, Tables 1.4 and 1.13. https://www.nso.go.th/public/e-book/Statistical-Yearbook/SYB-2025/76/ and https://www.nso.go.th/public/e-book/Statistical-Yearbook/SYB-2025/93/
- **MORT08**: WHO Thailand identifies motorcycle users as the majority of road traffic deaths and identifies helmet use, impaired driving and speed as important road-safety factors. ### Definition conflict

HISO cause-coded death-registration rates and DDC surveillance/open-data deaths use different recording systems and denominators. In particular, DDC drowning dashboards and road-death surveillance should be reported separately from HISO/NSO mortality rates unless a matched definition and denominator are documented. No surveillance count has been substituted into `mortality-reference.json` as a rate.

### Simulation calibration method

- The direct all-cause age/sex input rate is 2023 NSO registered deaths divided by registered population for the matching NSO age band. Rates are flat within each reported band; ages 0–4 and 65+ remain grouped as reported.
- A single multiplier per sex is found by binary search so the resulting synthetic life table reaches the 2024 NSO/Mahidol life-expectancy target (71.9 male, 80.0 female). This is labelled `calibrated`, not a new observed mortality rate. The other gender uses the arithmetic mean of the calibrated male/female hazards; that is a `design` approximation.
- Cause selection is conditional on a death. HISO's 2023 sex-specific cause rates provide the cause weights; any remainder against NSO all-cause crude rates is grouped as `other`. The known age-sex observations refine only where a matching source age band is present; otherwise cause share is held constant across source age bands. This is a deliberate `calibrated` simplification.
- For childhood drowning, the source's male 5–14 rate is used when available; health-zone drowning rates affect cause weight only where the report provides a zone value. Missing zone/cause values fall back to the national cause weight. Seasonality is not separately modeled per year; DDC reports seasonal clustering, but the game's turn is yearly.
- Player flags and stat-based changes in `balance.mortality` are `design` modifiers. WHO/DDC sources support the direction of some exposures, not these magnitudes. Combined modifiers are capped by the documented design ceiling. Auto-only simulations are reported separately and never used to recalibrate the mortality baseline.
- The reported provenance percentage counts the five operational parameter groups in `mortality-reference.json`, each scalar in `balance.mortality`, and `balance.maxAge` (20 entries total). Current values are 15% `sourced`, 10% `calibrated`, and 75% `design`; this counts model parameters, not deaths or underlying data points.
